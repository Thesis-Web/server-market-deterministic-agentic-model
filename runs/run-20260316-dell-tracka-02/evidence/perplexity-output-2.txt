Server/platform reliability & vendor preference (buyer evidence proxies)
ITIC – 2023 Global Server Hardware, Server OS Reliability Survey

Evidence: ITIC’s 2023 Global Server Hardware, Server OS Reliability Survey (PDF) reports that for the fifteenth year in a row, IBM Z and Power Systems servers “dominated the reliability and security landscape,” and that among x86 vendors Lenovo ThinkSystem servers achieved very high reliability, with 94% of Lenovo ThinkSystem enterprises attaining five–seven nines of reliability. It reports that Dell PowerEdge servers improved reliability to 24 minutes of unplanned per-server annual outage time in the 2023 survey, two minutes better than in 2022. These figures are based on a global survey of corporate respondents (sample size in 2023 report: not fully visible in excerpt; ITIC site indicates its surveys cover hundreds to low-thousands of enterprises).

Publisher: ITIC (Information Technology Intelligence Consulting)

Date: 2023 (report; ITIC reports page lists 2023 surveys).

ITIC – 2024–2025 Global Server Hardware, Server OS Reliability Survey

Evidence: 2024–2025 ITIC report notes that IBM Z, Power Systems and Lenovo ThinkSystem “for the sixteenth consecutive year” dominate reliability and security. The excerpt states that Dell PowerEdge servers improved reliability again, registering 20 minutes of unplanned per-server annual outage time and that this is a reduction of four minutes compared to prior results. This survey polled 1,950 global businesses from February–November 2024, updated April 2025.

Publisher: ITIC

Date: 2024 main fielding; updated April 2025.

G2. Enterprise server customer pain points – published research
Hardware reliability and MTTR / downtime
ITIC – 2023 Global Server Hardware, Server OS Reliability Survey

Evidence: The 2023 ITIC report lists per‑platform uptime and annual per-server unplanned downtime; for example, Lenovo ThinkSystem x86 servers have a 94% majority attaining five–seven nines of reliability; Dell PowerEdge servers average 24 minutes of unplanned per server annual outage time in the survey, down from 26 minutes in 2022. ITIC also notes that respondents reported “the most pronounced reliability declines in aged servers over 3½ years old,” and that many firms only upgrade “as needed” when performance degrades, causing freezes and crashes; aged servers are “much more prone to hard drive and component failures.”
​

Publisher: ITIC

Date: 2023

ITIC – 2024–2025 Global Server Hardware, Server OS Reliability Survey

Evidence: The 2024–2025 ITIC report states that IBM Z, IBM Power Systems and Lenovo ThinkSystem “dominated the reliability and security landscape,” and that Dell PowerEdge servers “also improved their reliability, registering 20 minutes of unplanned per server annual outage time,” four minutes better than before. It attributes downtime causes largely to security incidents and human error (84% cite security as top cause of unplanned downtime; 67% cite human error).

Publisher: ITIC

Date: Survey fielded February–November 2024, updated April 2025.

Lifecycle / refresh and aging hardware issues
Evidence: ITIC 2023 report notes that respondents commonly retain servers four, five, or even six years, especially SMBs, and that this correlates with “pronounced reliability declines” due to failure to upgrade/retrofit for mission‑critical workloads; aged servers are more prone to hard drive and component failures, and many customers do not right‑size servers for increased, memory‑intensive workloads.
​

Publisher: ITIC

Date: 2023
​
