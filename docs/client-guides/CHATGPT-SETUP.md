# CHATGPT PLUS — Setup and Usage Guide

## Your Final Compilation Tool

---

## What ChatGPT does in this system

ChatGPT is your editor and document compiler. After Claude has completed
the research analysis and built the strategy brief, you take that draft to
ChatGPT to polish it into a clean, professionally formatted final document.

ChatGPT is excellent at taking complex, structured content and making it
read like a high-quality professional deliverable. It handles document
flow, formatting consistency, and readability in a way that complements
Claude's analytical depth.

---

## One-time setup (10 minutes)

### Step 1 — Create your compilation Project

1. Log into chat.openai.com with your Plus account
2. In the left sidebar, click **Projects** (or the folder icon)
3. Click **+ New project**
4. Name it: `[company]-[topic]-compiler`
   Example: `dell-server-compiler`
5. Open the project

### Step 2 — Set the project instructions

Inside the project, find **Customize** or **Project Instructions**.
Copy the block below and paste it exactly:

---

```
You are a professional document compiler and editor. Your role is to take
analysis and research content provided to you and compile it into a clean,
polished, professional final document.

Your job is formatting and compilation — not research, analysis, or
adding new content.

Rules you must follow:
- Do not add new facts, claims, or analysis that are not in the content
  you receive
- Preserve all evidence tier labels (T1, T2, T3, T4, T5) exactly as written
- Preserve all [IP-INSERT: ...] placeholder blocks exactly as written —
  do not remove, summarize, or fill them in
- Preserve all sections marked [T5 inference] or [INFERENCE — not sourced]
  exactly as written — these labels are important and intentional
- Preserve the END-USER IP NOTES section at the bottom of every document
- Structure the document clearly with professional headers, logical flow,
  and consistent formatting
- Output the complete compiled document in a single response

You are the packaging layer. The content was built by analysts. Your job
is to make it read beautifully.
```

---

### Step 3 — You are ready

That is all the setup required for ChatGPT.

---

## How to use ChatGPT for compilation

Claude will tell you when it is time to take the draft to ChatGPT.
At that point:

1. Claude will give you the draft document text
2. Copy it entirely
3. Open your ChatGPT compilation project
4. Paste the transfer message that Claude gives you
   (Claude will prepare this for you — it will include the draft text)
5. ChatGPT will return a compiled, polished document
6. Copy the entire ChatGPT response
7. Open a plain text editor
8. Paste it and save as: `output_compiled.txt`
9. Return to Claude and upload it

Claude will then take the compiled document and generate your final PDF.

---

## Important: You do not use ChatGPT for the PDF

The final PDF is generated by Claude, not ChatGPT. This is intentional —
Claude has a reliable PDF generation capability that does not depend on
ChatGPT's download features. Once you return the compiled text to Claude,
Claude handles the PDF.

---

## What ChatGPT is NOT used for in this system

- Primary research (that is Perplexity and Grok)
- Analysis or strategy (that is Claude)
- PDF generation (that is Claude)

ChatGPT is specifically the compilation and editorial layer. Keep it focused
on that role and it will produce excellent results.

---

## If ChatGPT modifies the content unexpectedly

Occasionally ChatGPT may condense or reword content in ways that lose
important detail. If you notice the compiled output looks significantly
shorter than the input, or if labels or placeholder blocks are missing:

Tell ChatGPT: _"Please redo the compilation. You removed some sections
that need to be preserved. Specifically, you must keep all [IP-INSERT: ...]
blocks, all evidence tier labels, and all sections marked as inference.
Please include all content from the original."_

If it continues to drop content, return to Claude with what you have and
Claude will work with it.
