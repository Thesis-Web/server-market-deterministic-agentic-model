## Read this before you begin

This document explains the data risks involved in using AI tools for market
research. It is written in plain language for professionals who are not

This system is designed to help you manage them, but it cannot eliminate them.

This is not a disclaimer to ignore. It is practical, actionable guidance

---

## What actually happens when you type something into an AI tool

When you send a message to Claude, ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Grok, your text:

1. Travels over the internet to servers operated by the AI company
2. Is processed by their systems
3. May be stored by the company
4. May be reviewed by company employees for safety and quality purposes
5. Depending on your subscription terms, may be used to train future AI models

**This is true even when you delete the conversation.**

The moment you type something and send it, it has left your control.

---

## What this means for your company data

Any information you share with these tools should be treated as if you are
sharing it with an outside vendor under no confidentiality agreement.

**High risk — do not share without legal review:**
- Unreleased product roadmaps or launch dates
- Non-public financial data, revenue figures, or projections
- Merger, acquisition, or partnership discussions
- Customer lists, customer contracts, or customer usage data
- Personnel information, compensation, or HR matters
- Pricing strategies that are not publicly known
- Trade secrets or proprietary methods
- Information subject to NDA or legal hold

**Lower risk — generally acceptable with care:**
- Publicly available market data you are asking Claude to analyze
- Research questions about competitors' publicly known products
- Industry trends and publicly reported news
- Your own professional judgment about public information

## The specific tools and their data policies

### Claude (Anthropic)
Claude Pro conversations may be reviewed by Anthropic employees. By default
on the paid plan, Anthropic states that it does not use your conversations
to train models, but terms can change. Check: anthropic.com/legal/privacy

### ChatGPT (OpenAI)
ChatGPT Plus has a setting to opt out of training data use — verify it is
turned on before use. Find it in Settings → Data Controls → Improve the
model for everyone → Off.
Check: openai.com/policies/privacy-policy

### Perplexity
Perplexity Pro conversations may be stored and used according to their
privacy policy. Enterprise plans offer stronger protections.
Check: perplexity.ai/privacy

### Grok (xAI)
Grok is operated by xAI. Conversations on X/Grok are subject to X's privacy
terms. This is the tool with the most opaque data handling.
Check: x.ai/legal/privacy-policy

**These policies change over time. Verify current terms before sharing
sensitive data.**

## How this research system manages the risk

This system is designed with a separation of roles specifically to manage
data exposure:

**Perplexity and Grok** are used only for public market research — they
search publicly available information. If you follow the workflow correctly,
you should not need to share any proprietary data with these tools.

**Claude** is your analysis hub. The system is designed so that Claude
works primarily with the research data that Perplexity and Grok return
(which is all public information) plus the general context you provide
about your research goals.

**ChatGPT** handles only document compilation — working with material
Claude has already synthesized.

**The safest version of this system** involves no proprietary data at all.
The research outputs already contain the public evidence layer. Internal data
can be added manually to the IP-INSERT placeholder sections in your final PDF
without going through any AI tool.

---

## The IP-INSERT placeholders are your safe alternative

Throughout your research brief, you will see blocks like this:

```
[IP-INSERT: dell_win_loss_data
 POC-BEST-GUESS: [model inference]
 OVERRIDE: Replace with your internal win/loss data before publish]
```

These blocks are specifically designed so you can incorporate internal data
without sharing it with AI tools. You can:

1. Fill these sections in manually in the PDF or Word document
2. Have a trusted internal person fill them in
3. Share only specific, already-public information through the AI workflow

This approach gives you a professional deliverable that incorporates your
internal knowledge while keeping that knowledge off AI servers.

## If you must share internal data with Claude

If you decide the research benefit outweighs the risk and you choose to
share internal data:

1. Use only Claude — it has the strongest enterprise data commitments
   among the four tools
2. Check that your Claude account is on the Pro or Team plan (not free)
3. Verify the current data policy at anthropic.com/legal/privacy before
   each session where sensitive data is involved
4. Never share data that is under legal hold, NDA, or subject to
   regulatory reporting
5. Inform your legal and compliance team before sharing anything
   material to a regulatory filing or transaction
6. Consider using Claude's built-in project system (not an API integration)
   which has cleaner data handling than some third-party integrations

## What to do if your company has an IT or security policy

Many companies have policies about approved AI tools. Before using this
system with any company data, check with your IT or legal department.

Questions to ask them:
- Which AI tools are approved for business use?
- What categories of company information may be shared with external tools?
- Is there an approved enterprise AI solution (e.g., Microsoft Copilot,
  Google Workspace AI) that I should use instead for internal data?

This system can be adapted to work within enterprise AI policies.
Contact the system provider if you need guidance.
